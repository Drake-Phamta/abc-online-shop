# Product image credits

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons; each licence was read from the Commons API (`LicenseShortName`) on download.
Every photo was cropped, background-removed and placed on a white 800x800 canvas (JPEG, quality 85); a soft
contact shadow was added. No other retouching. Where the licence is CC BY or CC BY-SA, the author and licence
below must stay with the image, and CC BY-SA images stay under the same licence
(CC BY 2.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/, CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/,

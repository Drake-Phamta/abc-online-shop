5p("This essay followed ABC Online Shop from a list of business complaints to running code. Requirement "
6 "determination modelled the business in BPMN and ArchiMate, reduced seven problems to three root causes and "
7 "produced 14 stakeholder needs, 28 functional and 10 non-functional requirements with priorities, acceptance "
8 "criteria and traceability. Analysis turned them into 21 use cases with a detailed diagram for each functional "
9 "area, activity diagrams with swimlanes, a level 1 data flow diagram, sequence diagrams with boundary, control and "
10 "entity objects and a data model in three levels. The AI chapter treated the AI search as a component of the "
11 "information system like any other: it has stakeholders, use cases, a context and a data flow diagram, rules, "
12 "risks and a measured quality (hit@1 of 0.97 against 0.23 for the keyword search on 30 labelled queries, and 93% "
13 "of 84 test photos found first). Design carried the revised analysis model into a database of 20 tables that "
14 "Visual Paradigm generated from the physical model, and into 81 classes in three layers (73 in Java, 8 in the "
15 "browser) whose skeleton Visual Paradigm also generated; the code implements nine use cases of the purchase chain "
16 "and the AI search on that database and passes 24 unit and integration tests, 36 API checks and 30 end-to-end "
17 "checks in a browser. All 42 diagrams live in one Visual Paradigm project.")
18p("Three lessons stand out for me. The first is that most problems are cheapest to fix where they are created: a "
19 "requirement with a trigger and a fit criterion (FR09, FR16) travelled through every later phase without trouble, "
20 "while the vague first drafts of Section 1.14 would have produced vague classes and untestable code. The second "
21 "is that the models check each other, and that the check can be automated. The class model found the product "
22 "problem that the use cases hid; a script that compares the design classes with the compiled code found an "
23 "operation the code never called and a business rule repeated in a screen; the code that Visual Paradigm generated "
24 "from the model found navigable compositions and a type name that pointed to the wrong class; and the end-to-end "
25 "run found a page that a slow request could replace. None of these would have been found by looking at one model "
26 "alone. The third is the lesson of the lecturer's notes on AI in e-commerce: an AI-enabled shop is a complete "
27 "information system, not only a model. In the AI search the ranking itself is a small part, and the parts that "
28 "decide whether the feature is trustworthy are ordinary analysis and design: who may see which data, what happens "
29 "when the model is wrong or unavailable, how the customer sees why a product was found, and how the result is "
30 "measured.")
31p("If I had another month, I would implement the remaining use cases (registration, reviews, promotions and "
32 "reports) in the same three layers, move the database to PostgreSQL with full-text search, connect a real "
33 "payment provider through the CardProvider interface with a record of every request, and give the photo search a "
34 "learned image model behind the same ImageIndex interface, measured on the same 84 photos and then on search "
35 "success and conversion with real customers. The structure built during this semester should make each of those "
36 "changes a local one, and that, more than any single diagram, is what I take away from the course.")

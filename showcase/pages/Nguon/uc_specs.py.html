47 ["Item", "Description"],
48 ["Use case name", "Make Payment (UC08), an extension of UC07 at the extension point \"card payment chosen\""],
49 ["Primary actor", "Customer"],
50 ["Supporting actors", "Payment Provider, simulated inside the application: a card number that ends in 0002 is declined and any other valid number is approved"],
51 ["Goal", "Pay an order by card and have the provider's result recorded against that order"],
52 ["Trigger", "The customer has placed an order with the payment method card, and the checkout opens its payment page; the Pay now button of the order page opens it again later"],
53 ["Preconditions", "The customer is logged in; an order of the customer is PENDING_PAYMENT and the stock of its lines is reserved"],
54 ["Postconditions", "A payment record holds the result, APPROVED or DECLINED, with the card holder, the last four digits, the expiry and, for an approved card, the provider's token; the order history has an entry for it. The order is PAID after an approved result and stays PENDING_PAYMENT after a declined one. The full card number and the CVV are never stored"],
55 ["Main flow", "1. The customer enters the card holder, the card number, the expiry as MM/YY and the CVV and confirms. 2. The system checks the card data: holder, number format and Luhn checksum, expiry date and CVV (A2). 3. The system loads the order, which must be the customer's own (BR08) and PENDING_PAYMENT, and sends its reference and its stored total to the provider; the amount never comes from the request. 4. The provider approves the payment and returns a token. 5. In a second transaction the system loads the order again, records the payment as APPROVED with the last four digits and the token, and sets the order PAID (BR07). 6. The system shows the order, which is now PAID."],
56 ["Alternative flows", "A1 The card is declined: the payment is recorded as DECLINED, the order stays PENDING_PAYMENT with its stock reserved, and the page offers another card; the customer may also cancel the order in UC09. A2 The card data is invalid: refused with a message before the provider is called, and nothing is stored. A3 The order is not waiting for a card payment, for example already PAID or CANCELLED: refused (BR07). A4 The order belongs to another customer or does not exist: the answer is not found (BR08)."],
57 ["Exceptions", "The order was cancelled while the provider was answering: the second transaction refuses to record the payment (BR07) and the customer sees the refusal. The second transaction fails for another reason: the payment is not recorded, the order stays PENDING_PAYMENT and the customer sees a general error message. When the provider had approved, both cases leave a charge that the shop has not recorded; a real system closes this gap with an outbox table and a reconciliation job (Chapter 6)."],
58 ["Business rules", "BR07 an order becomes PAID only after an approved provider result, and only the card holder, the last four digits, the expiry and the token are stored. BR08 own orders only.

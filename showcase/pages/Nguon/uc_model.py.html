60p("The include and extend relationships needed the most care, and one of them changed after the design of Chapter 5. "
61 "UC07 Place Order includes UC21 Validate Cart, because the cart is always checked before confirmation (FR09). UC20 "
62 "Apply Promotion extends UC07 at the extension point \"promotion code entered\", because the extra behaviour only "
63 "happens when the customer selects an eligible promotion. In Assignment 02 I had also drawn UC08 Make Payment as an "
64 "include of UC07. The test I use for the choice is simple: if the base use case is incomplete without the other "
65 "behaviour, it is an include; if the base use case is complete and the other behaviour is added only under a "
66 "condition, it is an extend. Cash on delivery showed that the old choice failed this test: an order paid on "
67 "delivery is complete when it is placed, and the payment only runs when the customer chooses a card. UC08 therefore "
68 f"extends UC07 at the extension point \"card payment chosen\" with the condition payment method = CARD.")
